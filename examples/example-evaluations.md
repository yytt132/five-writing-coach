# Calibration Examples

These are intentionally short examples. They demonstrate how the same framework produces different judgments depending on purpose.

## Example 1 — Explainer writing

Text:

> When you are stressed, your body does not simply “feel bad.” It shifts into a state designed to help you respond to challenge. Hormones such as cortisol help mobilize energy. The problem begins when that emergency setting stays active too often or too long.

### Classification
- Primary: Explainer
- Supporting: Expert

### Sample score
- Common: 35/40
- Explainer-specific: 49/60
- Total: **84/100**

### Why
Strengths:
- intuitive opening;
- known-to-unknown sequence;
- mechanism before terminology.

Main weakness:
- “emergency setting” is useful but could imply an overly binary switch.

Rhetorical diagnosis:
- Logos: ★★★★☆
- Ethos: ★★★★☆
- Pathos: ★★★☆☆
- Kairos: ★★★★☆

Next practice target:
- add one concrete everyday example without making the explanation longer.

## Example 2 — Persuasive writing

Text:

> Universities should not respond to generative AI by banning it outright. A ban may reduce visible use, but it does not teach students how to evaluate generated claims, disclose assistance, or distinguish acceptable support from substitution. Assessment design should therefore shift from detecting tools to demonstrating learning.

### Classification
- Primary: Persuasive
- Supporting: Explainer + Expert

### Sample score
- Common: 36/40
- Persuasive-specific: 50/60
- Total: **86/100**

### Why
Strengths:
- clear thesis;
- reasons are connected to the conclusion;
- action direction is explicit.

Main weakness:
- the strongest counterargument — that bans can protect foundational skill acquisition in some settings — is not addressed.

Rhetorical diagnosis:
- Logos: ★★★★☆
- Ethos: ★★★★☆
- Pathos: ★★☆☆☆
- Kairos: ★★★★★

Next practice target:
- steelman one opposing argument before rebutting it.

## Example 3 — Practical writing

Weak version:

> 관련 자료의 원활한 취합을 위하여 협조하여 주시기 바랍니다.

Improved version:

> 첨부된 신청서를 작성해 10월 15일 18시까지 담당자 이메일로 제출해 주세요.

Why the second works better:
- the required action is explicit;
- the deadline is visible;
- the object to submit is named;
- the wording reduces interpretation burden.

Primary improvement:
- Kairos + action clarity.

## Example 4 — Literary vs. Expert scoring

Text:

> 휴대전화 화면을 세 번 켰지만 새 메시지는 없었다.

As Literary writing:
- strong concreteness and implied emotion.

As Expert writing:
- almost no claim, evidence, source, or verifiability.

The different scores are not contradictory. They reflect different purposes.
